Privacy in short

Pongui has no accounts and asks for no personal data.

  • Your progress, your settings and the levels you make are saved only in your browser, on your device. Clearing the site data erases them.
  • The site counts visits and a few game events (a level started or finished, the app installed, the editor opened, a level or a result shared) with Umami, a privacy-friendly statistics service. It uses no cookies, builds no profile and does not identify you. The numbers only show how many people play and where they get stuck.
  • If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, nothing is counted.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect them.
  • A shared level link contains only the level itself: its name and its map.
  • There are no ads and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Questions: hello@pongui.com.

Last updated: November 2026.
